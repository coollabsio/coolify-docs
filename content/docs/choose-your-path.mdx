# Choose your path

Coolify can be used in two ways: self-hosted, where you run and maintain the Coolify instance yourself, or Coolify Cloud, where the Coolify team runs and maintains the Coolify instance for you.

<CoolCallout icon={ArrowsRight} title="The short version">

Self-hosted and Cloud use the same Coolify features. The choice is only who operates the Coolify instance: you manage it yourself, or Coolify manages it for you.

</CoolCallout>

| Area | Self-hosted | Coolify Cloud |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Coolify instance | You install, update, backup, monitor, and restore the Coolify instance. | The Coolify team runs, updates, backs up, monitors, and restores the Coolify instance. |
| Your servers | You provide the servers and secure the operating system, SSH access, firewall, and network. | You still provide the servers and secure the operating system, SSH access, firewall, and network. |
| Your workloads | Your apps, databases, volumes, secrets, and runtime security remain your responsibility. | Your apps, databases, volumes, secrets, and runtime security remain your responsibility. |
| Coolify updates | You decide when to update Coolify and how to handle maintenance windows. | Coolify team applies control-plane updates for you. |
| Cost model | No Coolify license fee. You pay for your own infrastructure and operations time. | Paid managed service for the Coolify instance. You still pay for your own servers. |
| Feature set | All Coolify features. | Same as self-hosted. No Cloud-only feature paywall. |

<CoolCallout icon={Checklist2} title="Why this choice matters">

Both paths give you the same core product experience, but they differ in who is responsible for operating and maintaining the Coolify instance.

Coolify Cloud includes a monthly subscription for the managed instance, while self-hosted lets you run it yourself without recurring platform fees.

</CoolCallout>

<CoolCallout icon={Warning22} title="Still unsure?" type="warn">

**Start with Self-hosted if you are comfortable operating the control plane.**

Self-hosted gives you the clearest ownership model from day one. Choose Cloud when you want the Coolify team to handle the instance maintenance for you.

</CoolCallout>
