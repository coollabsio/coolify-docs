# Manage members

Use the active team's **Members** page to review access, change another member's role or access, and revoke invitations that should no longer be accepted.

## Review the member list

1. Confirm that the intended team is active.
2. Select **Team** in the Coolify sidebar.
3. Select **Members**.

The table shows **Name**, **Email**, **Role**, and the actions your role permits. Owners and administrators also see **2FA** status and, under the email address of each Member, a summary of their access, such as `Access: Team-wide read (default)`. Use search and the role filter to narrow the list, and select a column header to sort it.

Review the list regularly and remove access that is no longer needed. A person's role applies only to this team; the same account can have a different role in another team.

---

## Change a member's role

Owners and Admins can change another member's role. The available actions depend on your role and the other person's current role.

| Your role | Role changes available |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Owner** | Change another Owner, Admin, or Member to any of the other roles. |
| **Admin** | Change an Admin to Member, or change a Member to Admin. Owners cannot be changed. |
| **Member** | No role-change actions. |

Find the member, open the `...` menu in their row, then select **Make owner**, **Make admin**, or **Make member**.

<Callout type="info" title="A lower role revokes team API tokens">
When you change a member to a lower role, Coolify revokes that user's API tokens for this team. The person must create replacement tokens if they still need API access. A change to a higher role keeps the tokens.
</Callout>

When an Owner or Admin becomes a Member, Coolify also resets their access to `Team-wide read (default)`.

The member list reloads after the change. Confirm that the **Role** column shows the new role.

You cannot change your own role from the member list. Ask another person with sufficient access to make the change.

---

## Change a Member's access

For a person with the **Member** role, open the `...` menu in their row and select **Edit access**. Choose a preset or set the scopes, then select **Save**.

Follow [Set member access](/core/team/members/set-member-access) for the access levels, presets, and what a Member with limited access can see.

---

## Review pending invitations

Owners and Admins can review **Pending invitations** below the invitation form.

Each row shows:

- **Email**: the invited email address
- **Method**: whether the invitation was created for email delivery or as a link
- **Role**: the role the person will receive, and for a Member a summary of their access
- **Link**: a copy button for the invitation link

Invitation links are valid for three days. A valid invitation for an email address must be accepted, revoked, or allowed to expire before another invitation can be created for that address.

---

## Revoke an invitation

Select the trash icon (**Revoke invitation**) in the row of an invitation that should no longer grant access. The **Revoke invitation?** dialog shows that the invitation link stops working. Select **Revoke invitation** to confirm.

Coolify removes the pending invitation. Confirm that its row disappears from **Pending invitations**. If the person still needs access, create a new invitation with the correct role.
