# Profile and account deletion

Open the user menu and select **Profile** to manage your account and [two-factor authentication](/core/security/authentication/2fa). Linked SSO identities are displayed here. When your email is managed by a provider, change it at the provider; Coolify does not allow editing it locally.

For an account with a linked OAuth identity, destructive actions use typed confirmation without requiring a Coolify password. Review every action listed by the confirmation before proceeding.

## Delete your account

Use the account-deletion action on **Profile** and complete the confirmation. Deleting an account:

- deletes teams where you are the only member
- removes you from all other teams
- revokes your API tokens and sessions

Deletion is blocked for the root user, while a team you own alone still has servers, projects, or Git sources, and when leaving a shared team would leave it without an administrator or owner.

Before retrying a blocked deletion, remove the remaining resources or arrange the required ownership and administration in each team. Account deletion is separate from removing a member through team administration; see [Remove a member](/core/team/members/remove-a-member).
