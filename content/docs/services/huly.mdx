## Upgrade to Huly v7

New services use Huly v0.7.426 with CockroachDB instead of MongoDB. An existing service keeps its image.

<Callout type="warning">

Do not upgrade from v6 to v7 directly.

</Callout>

1. Back up your data with `hardcoreeng/tool:v0.6.504 backup-all-to-dir`.
2. Deploy a new Huly v7 service.
3. Restore the data with `hardcoreeng/tool:s0.7.251 restore-from-v6-all`.

See the [official migration guide](https://github.com/hcengineering/huly-selfhost/blob/main/MIGRATION.md?utm_source=coolify.io).
