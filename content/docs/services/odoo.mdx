## Upgrade to Odoo 20

New services use Odoo 20. An existing service keeps its image until you change it in **Configuration > General > Edit Compose File**.

Odoo 20 needs PostgreSQL 16 or later. The community edition does not upgrade the database across major versions. Use [OpenUpgrade](https://github.com/OCA/OpenUpgrade?utm_source=coolify.io) or the [Odoo upgrade service](https://upgrade.odoo.com/?utm_source=coolify.io).
