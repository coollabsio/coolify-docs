# Airbyte

![Airbyte](/docs/images/services/airbyte-logo.svg)

## What is Airbyte?

Airbyte is an open source data replication platform and context layer for AI agents. Equip your agents to query, search, and act on your data in real-time, or replicate data from hundreds of sources into warehouses, lakes, and databases.

## Deployment Note

Airbyte has [deprecated Docker Compose deployments in favour of `abctl`](https://docs.airbyte.com/platform/deploying-airbyte/migrating-from-docker-compose).

As a result, this Coolify template is based on the community-maintained [airbyte-compose](https://github.com/nktnet1/airbyte-compose) repository rather than an officially maintained Docker Compose deployment.

## Usage

### Initial Setup

After deploying the template, open the Web UI link and complete the setup by entering your email address and company name.

You can then log in using the value set in the environment variable `SERVICE_PASSWORD_AIRBYTEADMIN`.

### Connecting to Other Databases on the Coolify Network

If you wish to connect to databases hosted on Coolify but external to the Airbyte service template, you will need to enable the option [Connect to Predefined Networks](https://coolify.io/docs/knowledge-base/docker/compose#connect-to-predefined-networks).

However, this may result in hostname conflicts with other services on the network. It is highly recommended that you [create a new Coolify destination](https://coolify.io/docs/knowledge-base/destinations/create) and deploy your Airbyte service and other databases to that destination instead.
