# systemprompt

## What is systemprompt?

systemprompt is the self-owned AI control plane: one binary and one Postgres that run and govern every model, agent, and tool call your team makes. Instead of renting a dashboard that sits as a black box between your engineers and their models, you own the gateway.

Every AI request and MCP tool call passes through a four-stage governance pipeline (scope check, secret scan, blocklist, rate limit) and lands in your PostgreSQL with a trace linking identity, agent, tool, result, and cost. Point any Anthropic-compatible client, including Claude Code, at the gateway and usage becomes something you can see, cap, and audit.

The Coolify template provisions the gateway from the official GHCR image plus a PostgreSQL 18 database. Database credentials and the JWT secret are generated automatically; supply at least one provider key (`ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, `OPENAI_API_KEY`, or `GEMINI_API_KEY`) before the first deploy.
