<img src="/docs/images/services/solidtime.svg" alt="solidtime Logo" />

## What is Solidtime?

solidtime is a modern open-source time tracker built for freelancers, agencies and
teams. You can track time on projects and tasks, manage clients and billable rates,
review your team's hours, and share report links with people outside your
organization.

The Coolify template ships the full stack: the app itself with its scheduler and
queue workers, a PostgreSQL database, and Gotenberg for PDF export. Passport keys
are generated automatically on first boot, and database migrations run on their
own, so a fresh deployment is ready to use as soon as it comes up.
