# One-click Service catalog

Browse the ready-made Docker Compose templates available under **Create New Resource** in Coolify. Select a template for its setup guide and service-specific notes.

On the **New Resource** page and in the global search, Coolify marks a service with an **Outdated** badge when its image has not been updated in the last 2 months. Only the main images of the service count, not its database or other helper images.
