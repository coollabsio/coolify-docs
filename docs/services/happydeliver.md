# happyDeliver

## What is happyDeliver?

happyDeliver is an open-source email deliverability testing platform that provides comprehensive analysis of your email configuration. It's a privacy-focused alternative to commercial deliverability testers, allowing you to test and validate your email infrastructure without sending data to third-party services.

## Requirements

happyDeliver requires **port 25** to be available and open to receive incoming SMTP connections for email deliverability testing. Ensure this port is not blocked by your firewall, ISP, cloud provider or used by another service.
